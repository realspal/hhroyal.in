The long sequence of foundation, ascendancy, fragmentation and renewal. All dates are approximate and may vary across scholarly reconstructions.

THE IMPERIAL ARCHIVEEST. c. 750 CE
A FOUR-CENTURY SEQUENCE

History across generations.

The timeline is a guide to change, not a claim that every ruler held the same territory or power. Inscriptions often preserve regnal years without an established era date.

c. 750–770The FoundationGopal unifies power in Bengal and establishes the ruling house.↗c. 770–850The Imperial AscendancyDharmapal and Devpal make eastern India a first-rank power.↗c. 850–980Continuity and ContractionA complex succession amid changing regional relationships.↗c. 980–1040The Mahipal RenewalMahipal I revives Pal authority in Bengal and Bihar.↗c. 1040–1080The Northern CrisisConflict with neighbours and the loss of Varendra.↗c. 1080–1130The RestorationRampal restores Varendra and presides over the late imperial age.↗c. 1130–1160+The Final CenturyThe decline of dynastic rule and the uncertain terminal succession.↗
